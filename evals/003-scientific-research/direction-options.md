# Design direction coaching record

## Recommended directions

### A. Journal Figure — recommended

Figure-led research presentation: white field, navy text, semantic blue/red
accents, panel labels, captions, citations, and one main visual per page.

### B. Clinical Evidence

More formal evidence board: cool blue-gray field, larger disclaimers, study
limitations, and stronger treatment/control comparison. Risk: less expressive
for a lab group meeting.

### C. Data Atlas

Dense specimen archive: multiple labeled panels, matrix views, and method
notations. Risk: can become too dense for a short 10-minute presentation.

## Selected direction lock

Select A because the audience needs a clear scientific narrative and the brief
requires figure captions, method stages, and restrained evidence presentation.

- Palette: paper `#F7F8FA`, navy `#1E293B`, control blue `#3B82F6`, treatment red `#C2414C`, muted `#64748B`
- Typography: readable sans, CJK fallback, no decorative title effects
- Page grammar: formal cover → question → methods → Figure 1 → discussion → references
- Motion: none; all meaning must survive static PNG/PDF
- Forbidden: KPI cards, hero marketing copy, gradient decorations, uncited claims,
  business feature grids, and false precision
