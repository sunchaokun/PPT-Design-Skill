# Brief acceptance contract

| ID | Requirement | Visible evidence expected in PNG | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Use a scientific, not commercial, paradigm | research question, methods, results, limitations, references | MUST |
| R2 | Explain the three method stages | readable sequence from preparation to modeling | MUST |
| R3 | Show the supplied control/treatment comparison | Figure 1 with 0.42 and 0.78, clearly labeled | MUST |
| R4 | Use figure numbering, captions, and source notes | Figure 1 label, caption, and source note are visible | MUST |
| R5 | Use semantic colors | blue control and red treatment are stable and explained | MUST |
| R6 | Six pages, 16:9, editable | structure check and native text/shape/chart objects | MUST |
| R7 | Avoid business patterns and unsupported claims | no KPI cards, sales CTA, invented dataset, or marketing arc | MUST |
| R8 | Static readability | no clipping, overlap, tiny body text, or dependence on animation | MUST |
