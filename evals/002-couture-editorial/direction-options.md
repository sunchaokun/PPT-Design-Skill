# Design direction coaching record

## Initial recommendation

The skill should not ask the novice user to choose a palette or layout. It
should recommend these three directions in plain language:

### A. Atelier Research — recommended

**Thesis:** a study of volume, material, and the body.

**Grammar:** asymmetric editorial grid, bone white and ink, serif display type,
material labels, silhouette crops, large quiet fields, thin rules and numbered
studies.

**Avoids:** product catalog cards, symmetric feature rows, sales copy, and
generic luxury gradients.

**Tradeoff:** requires precise copy and disciplined image crops; intentionally
feels more like a private editorial than a campaign advertisement.

### B. Nocturnal Runway

**Thesis:** movement and reveal under controlled light.

**Grammar:** near-black ground, cinematic crops, oversized type, sharp accent
line, sequence-like page transitions.

**Risk:** can become too campaign-like and reduce the research character.

### C. Material Archive

**Thesis:** a curatorial index of craft and provenance.

**Grammar:** specimen labels, numbered material studies, texture panels,
restrained grid, captions, and catalog rhythm.

**Risk:** more archival and less emotionally immediate.

## User-facing recommendation

```text
推荐 A：Atelier Research。
它最适合“高定视觉研究册”，会让观众感到克制、专业、具有艺术指导感，
同时能表达体积、材质和手工。它不会做成普通产品画册或三栏卖点页。
代价是需要更精炼的文案和更严格的图片裁切。
```

## Visual direction lock

- Mode: Build Mode
- Visual thesis: the garment is a moving study of body, volume, and handwork
- Audience promise: private, intelligent, tactile, editorial
- Palette: bone `#F3EEE7`, ink `#151515`, oxblood `#5B1720`, muted stone `#B8AEA4`
- Typography: large serif display for titles, restrained sans for metadata
- Grid: asymmetric 12-column logic, generous outer margins, intentional offsets
- Image treatment: large crops, full-bleed or edge-to-edge blocks, no stretched images
- Page rhythm: manifesto → silhouette → material → gesture → final fitting
- Must-have signatures: numbered studies, thin rules, material labels, varied scale
- Forbidden: card grids, product feature bullets, centered corporate layouts,
  generic gold-on-black luxury styling, unrelated decorative effects
