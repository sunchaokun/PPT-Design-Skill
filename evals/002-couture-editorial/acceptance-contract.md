# Brief acceptance contract

| ID | Requirement | Visible evidence expected in PNG | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | This is couture editorial, not a product catalog | page grammar uses studies, labels, crops, and editorial pacing | MUST |
| R2 | Express body, volume, material, handwork, and motion | those ideas appear as page themes or visual evidence | MUST |
| R3 | Use Atelier Research direction | bone/ink palette, asymmetry, serif display, rules, numbering | MUST |
| R4 | Six pages, 16:9, English-led | structure report confirms six slides and readable title system | MUST |
| R5 | Image treatment is intentional | crops support the narrative and are never stretched or irrelevant | MUST |
| R6 | Avoid generic card-template composition | no repeated cards or business feature grids | MUST |
| R7 | Editorial hierarchy and whitespace are intentional | sparse pages feel composed, not empty; dense pages remain legible | MUST |
| R8 | Important text remains editable | text and metadata are native PowerPoint objects | MUST |

## Pass rule

Every `MUST` row must pass after PNG review. A visually attractive but generic
product brochure fails R1, R3, or R6.
