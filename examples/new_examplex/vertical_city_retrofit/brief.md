# Flagship Case 007: Vertical City Retrofit

## Brief

Create a 14-page, 16:9 editorial strategy deck titled **Vertical City
Retrofit** for urban-development leaders, architects, public-sector decision
makers, and institutional investors.

The deck explains how aging high-rise housing can become a more resilient
vertical city through a coordinated retrofit of envelope, energy, mobility,
shared space, and community services.

## Core thesis

**The next city is not built from scratch. It is upgraded in place.**

The story must move from the visible problem to the hidden system, then from
system diagnosis to a phased retrofit strategy and an investment decision.

## Content boundary

The case will use public, source-backed references for building age, energy,
housing, and retrofit principles. Any project economics, phasing targets, or
illustrative performance curves will be explicitly marked as scenario design,
not as measured outcomes.

## Competitive objective

This case must demonstrate a broader visual vocabulary than the previous
examples: editorial photography, architectural section, exploded axonometric,
systems map, data chart, phased timeline, option matrix, and final decision
framework. No repeated card-grid layout.

## Audience takeaway

A retrofit is investable when the building is treated as a connected urban
system rather than a façade-renovation project.
