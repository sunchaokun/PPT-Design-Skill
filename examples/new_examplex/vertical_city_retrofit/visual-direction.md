# Visual direction — Vertical City Retrofit

## Positioning

Architectural editorial meets infrastructure strategy: tactile enough to feel
like a city, analytical enough to support a capital decision.

## Palette

- Paper: warm mineral `#F1EEE8`
- Graphite: `#20272B`
- Brick: `#B85C42`
- Oxidized green: `#597461`
- Systems blue: `#1F6F8B`
- Rule line: `#C9C2B8`

## Graphic language

- Use thin construction lines, section cuts, datum lines, and numbered callouts.
- Use photographs as evidence of lived conditions, not decorative backgrounds.
- Use native diagrams for systems and phasing; use image crops only when the
  source image carries the argument.
- Use typography as a navigation system: chapter labels, coordinates, and
  concise editorial captions.
- Avoid rounded rectangles, pill tags, dashboard-like cards, and identical
  repeated modules.

## Cover direction

Create one conceptual hero image of a mature high-rise at dusk, showing a
sectional cutaway with warm occupied rooms, planted terraces, and a subtle blue
energy / mobility network. Keep the left third quiet for title placement.

## QA bar

Every page must have a distinct compositional idea. The deck should read as a
designed publication with a decision spine, not as fourteen slides using one
template.
