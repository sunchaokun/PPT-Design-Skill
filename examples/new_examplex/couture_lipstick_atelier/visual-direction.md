# Visual Direction — COUTURE COLOR / OBJECTS OF DESIRE

The deck borrows the **observable qualities** of a flagship fashion editorial:
an image-led first read, generous but purposeful darkness, optical tension
between oversized type and small notation, and a restrained luxury material
palette. It does not imitate an existing brand or campaign.

The cover is a full-bleed beauty editorial: an application gesture sits on the
right while a long, soft left-to-right oxblood gradient creates a calm title
field without a visible dividing edge. The same model reappears on the
proposition page in a distinctly closer, lowered-gaze application frame,
making the outcome of the product tangible before the deck moves into object,
pigment, and ritual.

The recurring graphic device is a delicate native SVG gesture sweep, used only
where it has semantic work to do: on the application page. It is never treated
as a decorative logo or forced into unrelated layouts.

Large empty zones are reserved for either a product silhouette to breathe or a
single editorial statement to land. Fine champagne rules, small catalogue-like
labels, and numbered pages establish precision. Native PowerPoint geometry
carries the product, shade, and timeline systems; the generated photographs
only carry imagery that cannot be expressed more honestly as editable shapes.
