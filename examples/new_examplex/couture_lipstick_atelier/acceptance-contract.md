# Acceptance Contract

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Rendered evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Flagship rather than generic cosmetics deck | MUST | Every page has a composed focal point, editorial hierarchy, and intentional whitespace. |
| R2 | Premium high-couture product language | MUST | Oxblood/bone/champagne system, precise rules, restrained serif display type, tactile product photography. |
| R3 | Ten varied pages in English | MUST | Ten rendered pages with distinct narrative roles and no Chinese copy. |
| R4 | Photography-grade asset treatment | MUST | Original product photography is cover-fit, tonally consistent, and is never used as a stretched placeholder. |
| R4a | Show the product in use | MUST | Cover and proposition page visibly pair the couture lipstick with an editorial model and a credible final-application gesture. |
| R5 | Editable presentation structure | MUST | Headlines, swatches, product geometry, timeline, and diagrams remain native editable PowerPoint objects. |
| R6 | Advanced SVG enriches rather than overwhelms | SHOULD | The native SVG gesture traces on slide 6 are legible, low-opacity, and materially clarify the application narrative. |
| R7 | No unsupported commercial claims | MUST | All framing remains conceptual; no fabricated sales, testing, or market metrics. |
