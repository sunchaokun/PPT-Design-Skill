# COUTURE COLOR — Brief

**Audience:** portfolio reviewers, premium beauty and fashion clients
**Format:** 10-slide English editorial concept case
**Purpose:** demonstrate a flagship luxury-product presentation system, not a commercial launch plan.

## Visual thesis

Treat lipstick as a couture object: a precise red form, designed through light,
material, gesture, and ritual. The deck should feel like a fashion editorial
spread rather than a conventional cosmetics pitch.

## Locked direction

- **Mood:** nocturnal, tactile, restrained, exacting.
- **Palette:** oxblood, black cherry, bone, warm champagne metal, smoked rose.
- **Type:** Georgia for the editorial display voice; Arial Narrow / Arial for
  measured information.
- **Grid:** 0.56 in outer margin, 12-column rhythm, fine champagne rules.
- **Image treatment:** five original AI-generated editorial photographs,
  including two frames of the same model in a product-in-use beauty sequence;
  never stretched, with hard-edged crops, a locked tonal grade, and controlled
  negative space.
- **Design dials:** variance 7/10; motion 2/10; density 5/10.

## Page map

1. Cover — an editorial face, lipstick, and the colour object.
2. Proposition — colour as a couture gesture in use.
3. Chromatic wardrobe — an editable shade architecture.
4. Object grammar — an editable exploded product diagram.
5. Material choreography — pigment, lacquer, and metal.
6. The gesture — a typographic interpretation of application.
7. The collection — three tonal families as a product system.
8. The ritual — a retail / dressing-table moment.
9. The release — a non-commercial editorial sequence.
10. Coda — the collection's final point of view.
