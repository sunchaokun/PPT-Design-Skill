# Visual direction

## Direction: Red Ink / Infrastructure Ledger

This is a premium editorial data report, not a dark dashboard. The visual
language combines financial-document precision with physical infrastructure
imagery expressed through geometry rather than stock photos.

- Warm paper background with deep ink typography;
- Cobalt for reported evidence and source-backed facts;
- Vermilion for constraint, risk, and capital pressure;
- Acid green for operating leverage and validated flow;
- Hairline rules, ledger labels, oversized numerals, and editorial captions;
- Small monospace source labels keep the evidence layer visible;
- Native charts, stacked bars, flow diagrams, and decision matrices;
- No gradients, generic KPI cards, or unqualified “market size” claims.

## Dials

- Variance: 8/10 — chart pages, stack maps, matrices, flow diagrams, and editorial statements;
- Motion: 2/10 — calm report rhythm with directional arrows only;
- Density: 8/10 — information-rich, but one clear argument per page.

## Visual thesis

**The bottleneck is part of the market.**

Every page should distinguish a reported fact, an interpretation, and an
operator decision.
