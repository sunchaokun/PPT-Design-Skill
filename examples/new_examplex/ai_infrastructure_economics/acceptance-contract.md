# Acceptance contract

| ID | Level | Requirement | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | MUST | 12-page, 16:9 editable PPTX | Structural inspection |
| R2 | MUST | Data story moves from capex to constraints to decisions | Slides 1–12 |
| R3 | MUST | Public figures are labeled by source and period | Slides 3–5 and footnotes |
| R4 | MUST | At least three editable chart/diagram structures | Slides 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10 |
| R5 | MUST | Illustrative analysis is visibly distinguished from reported facts | Source labels and accent semantics |
| R6 | MUST | No fake cross-company ranking or invented customer claims | Content audit |
| R7 | MUST | Premium editorial data language, not generic dashboard cards | PNG review |
| R8 | SHOULD | Page architecture varies intentionally | Cover, stack, chart, flow, matrix, scorecard, close |
| R9 | NICE | Final page gives operators a usable decision model | Slide 12 |
