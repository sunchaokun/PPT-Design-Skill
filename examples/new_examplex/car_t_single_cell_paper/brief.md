# Flagship Case 006: Single-cell CAR T Atlas

## Source paper

Bai, Z., Feng, B., McClory, S. E. et al. **Single-cell CAR T atlas reveals type
2 function in 8-year leukaemia remission.** Nature 634, 702–711 (2024).
DOI: 10.1038/s41586-024-07762-w.

## Brief

Create a 12-page, 16:9 English academic research deck for a lab meeting,
journal club, or thesis-style research discussion. The deck should explain the
paper's evidence chain from clinical persistence to cell-state atlas,
type-2-function mechanism, serum cytokines, and in-vivo validation.

## Non-negotiable evidence rules

- Use the paper's reported cohort sizes, cell counts, persistence groups,
  cytokine measurements, doses, and animal-study details only;
- Label all redraws as “redrawn from Fig. X” and retain the paper citation;
- Do not fabricate a new dataset, effect size, p-value, or clinical threshold;
- Separate association, mechanistic evidence, and preclinical intervention;
- State the paper's own boundary: type-2 functionality is promising, but the
  optimal clinical level remains to be established.

## Required capability proof

- Figure-led academic composition rather than business cards;
- Editable cohort diagrams, study design, bar charts, flow diagrams, and
  evidence ladders;
- Clear captions, source notes, and figure references;
- A restrained biological color semantic: type 2 / durable persistence / IL-4
  in red-orange; comparison or baseline in indigo; mechanism in teal.
