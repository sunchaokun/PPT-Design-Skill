# Acceptance contract

| ID | Level | Requirement | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | MUST | 12-page, 16:9 editable PPTX | Structural inspection |
| R2 | MUST | All content derives from the named Nature paper | Paper citation and source notes |
| R3 | MUST | Cohort, cell count, persistence groups, and intervention details are accurate | Slides 3, 5, 8–10 |
| R4 | MUST | Association, mechanism, and preclinical evidence are visually separated | Slides 5, 7, 9, 11 |
| R5 | MUST | Figure references and captions are visible | Slides 4–10 |
| R6 | MUST | No invented dataset, p-value, clinical threshold, or efficacy claim | Content audit |
| R7 | MUST | Figure-led academic visual language | PNG review |
| R8 | SHOULD | At least five distinct page architectures | Cover, cohort, atlas, chart, mechanism, intervention, evidence ladder |
| R9 | NICE | Final slide states the paper's contribution and boundary | Slide 12 |
