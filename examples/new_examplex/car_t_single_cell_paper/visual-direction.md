# Visual direction

## Direction: BioTech Blue Research Editorial

This revision treats the case as a high-density life-science research report:
ice-blue field, deep navy typography, cobalt section structure, cyan data paths,
magenta type-2/persistence highlights, and restrained lime/orange validation cues.

- Four-zone grid on most pages: conclusion, figure, method/interpretation, boundary;
- Paper figures are evidence modules, not full-screen backgrounds;
- Figure area target is 25–45% per page, with 3–5 information units per page;
- UMAP, chord diagrams, heatmaps, time series, animal imaging and survival plots are
  used directly from Bai et al. (2024), with source labels;
- Editable text, arrows, metrics and mechanism summaries explain the original figures;
- Clinical association, cellular state, mechanism and preclinical intervention remain
  visibly distinct.

## Visual thesis

**A strong biotech story makes the evidence dense, legible and bounded.**
