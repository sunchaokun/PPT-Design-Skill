# Brief

**Topic:** Louvre Abu Dhabi by Jean Nouvel

**Audience:** Architecture and cultural-project portfolio reviewers.

**Scenario:** A flagship English-language case study that demonstrates high-end
editorial presentation design through a real international architecture project.

**Purpose:** Make the museum memorable as an atmospheric institution shaped by
light, water, geometry, and civic experience—not merely as an iconic object.

**Generation mode:** Build Mode with the public `pptx_designer` library.

**Editability:** All text and diagrams must remain editable in PowerPoint;
photography may remain raster imagery.
