templates. Coordinates are normalized to the 16:9 canvas.

## P01 — opening / quiet field with dominant mass

- Page job: establish the museum as a nocturnal cultural landmark.
- Primary structure: `c1-01`.
- Anchor: full-bleed night photograph with large editorial title.
- Zones: title-safe area at left; image field across the canvas; small metadata at bottom.
- Native plan: title, metadata, page number, and image as native objects.
- SVG assistance: none required for the primary message; star geometry is optional decoration.
- Avoid: equal cards, centered feature stack, text baked into the photograph.
